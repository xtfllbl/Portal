# Service-provider billing

Mode: Operate. Extend the incumbent PAYwizard Maintain / Portal interface, as explicitly requested after the completed requirements interview. This is a code-led extension of the existing billing system, not a new visual identity; no persistent workflow preference is inferred.

Operations configures provider fees, traces device events, reviews quarterly calculations and edits unpaid bills. Providers inspect final published charges and pay through the existing checkout. Preserve 34px / 11px black table headers, 40px form and 36px row actions, shared Poppins shell and Material Symbols Rounded. Reuse the existing searchable top-layer picker.

FIRST VIEWPORT: one aligned heading/action row, understated horizontal tabs, a single search/filter toolbar and compact invoice table. No metric-card dashboard or subtitle. Bill review uses a broad line-item table alongside a narrow, white final-amount summary; system comparison is disclosed in an operations-only tab.

Signature interaction: a quantity or amount adjustment immediately updates the final total and the difference from the original calculation; saving preserves audit history and the provider sees only the final bill. Unpaid revisions force checkout reconfirmation.

Desktop evidence: 1440×1000 and 1280×800. Initial incumbent evidence: /tmp/provider-billing-incumbent.png. Scope excludes production APIs, genuine payments and deployment.
