## Campaign coverage

Eight additional drafts use every new image across square full-screen fit, square full-screen crop, square idle-screen rotation, portrait posters, landscape offers, long-strip fit, long-strip crop, and mixed customer uploads. Select Q3min (480 × 480) in Terminal Preview to compare square-screen behavior; Q3V remains available for portrait comparisons. The model selector is a preview preference, not terminal targeting metadata.

The square idle-screen example uses the central advertising area while payment brands and the merchant footer remain visible. A square source therefore does not necessarily fill this area.

The sample catalog is appended once through `sampleCatalogVersion: 1`. Existing assets, edits, drafts, publications and assignments are preserved. New campaigns are unassigned drafts and do not claim terminals. Later reads do not restore samples deleted after this batch was applied.
