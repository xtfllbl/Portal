For a small, self-contained demo, clips were trimmed to 5–12.5 seconds, muted, resized with preserved proportions, and center-cropped where the demo aspect ratio differs from the source. Square crops cover Q3min (480 × 480); portrait, landscape, low-resolution and ultra-wide variants exercise the other media shapes. Six files use H.264 MP4 with fast-start metadata and two use VP9 WebM. All clips use 24 fps. JPEG covers are frames extracted at 1 second from their corresponding local video.

Playback uses these local files without external video requests. The media library and media picker show a cover; opening Media Preview or a video Campaign plays the actual clip.

## Campaigns and saved data

Six new, unassigned full-screen video drafts cover square full-frame, square crop, portrait coffee/coast, landscape city/pets, an ultra-wide loop and an eight-video mixed-format playlist. Videos remain separate from image playlists, following the existing Advertising Campaign rules.

The independent `sampleVideoCatalogVersion: 1` batch appends these assets and drafts to existing browsers without reapplying the image catalog. Existing uploads, image deletions, custom campaign edits, publications and assignments remain intact. Once saved, removing a video sample does not cause it to return on refresh.
