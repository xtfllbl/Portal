# Billing records can exist without merchant assignment

Operations needs to collect external customer payments without assigning the obligation to a merchant, so a Standalone Billing Record has no merchant assignment and supports both One-time and Fixed-term monthly payments. Merchant assignment and payment cadence are independent: this extends ADR 0003's merchant-only scope while retaining its shared billing state and single-record link boundary, and applies ADR 0002's finite installment contract to standalone billing as well. A link settles its existing obligation rather than creating a new obligation for each visitor; a separate customer charge requires a separate billing record.

Standalone bills are managed by operations alongside merchant bills and are excluded from merchant billing views and merchant selection. Creation requires no external customer profile; invoice numbers and billing notes identify the obligation, a recipient email is entered when using Send Link, and payer email is entered at checkout. General Billing and eSIM Billing, including their existing draft, expiry, currency and fixed-term collection rules, remain available for both assignments.
