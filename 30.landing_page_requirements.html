Requirement Flow

Suggested review order for product and operations validation.

1. Configure partner styleUse Partner Information to assign UPT or INTL PSP sharing landing page style.
2. Share the landing pageOpen the UPT form, the default INTL PSP form, or a fixed-channel INTL PSP link.
3. Capture merchant submissionCollect merchant contact, terminal SN, selected or fixed payment channel, files, or manual parameters.
4. Review recordsOperations use the unified Leads console tabs to separate UPT and INTL PSP submissions.
5. Inspect detailOpen the INTL PSP lead detail page for submitted parameters, attachments, and review notes.
